Here I replicate (or try to!) well-known psychophysics experiments, with vision-language models (for now, Qwen3-VL-2B) in the observer's chair.

  1. Pick a psychophysics experiment and summarize its main results with human observers.
  2. Recreate the stimulus set and test VLMs with the same task.
  3. Compare humans to VLMs.
